Chapter Help

الْكَلَامُ هُوَ اللَّفْظُ الْمُرَكَّبُ الْمُفِيدُ بِالْوَضْعِ.
Speech is an utterance that is composed, conveys a complete meaning, and follows Arabic usage.
وَأَقَلُّ مَا يَتَأَلَّفُ مِنْهُ: اسْمَانِ، نَحْوُ: زَيْدٌ قَائِمٌ؛ أَوْ فِعْلٌ وَاسْمٌ، نَحْوُ: قَامَ زَيْدٌ.
Its smallest complete form is two nouns, or a verb together with its subject.
إِنْ قَامَ زَيْدٌ
This is deliberately incomplete: the condition “If Zayd stood…” still needs an answer to complete the meaning.
Source: al-Mutammimah, “Speech and what it is composed of,” printed page 19. English explanations are prepared for Miftāḥ al-ʿIlm.
